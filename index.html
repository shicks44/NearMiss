THE WHOLE THING

Close counts.
Going over doesn’t.

Each round gives you a category and a number. Name an item that gets as close as possible without exceeding it.

Your score is simple: your value ÷ the target × 100. Go over and it’s a bust. You get one locked-in answer per round.

The same five rounds are waiting for everyone today. A fresh set drops at midnight UTC.
A NOTE ON THE NUMBERS

Good guesses.
Grounded numbers.

The launch edition uses a small, hand-curated set of stable reference values, with units and sources shown after each answer. Values are snapshots and may vary by source or measurement method.

This first release keeps your progress in your browser. Daily rounds are deterministic, but answer validation and scoring happen on-device, so they are for fun rather than a tamper-proof leaderboard.

More datasets and a server-backed daily schedule can be added without changing the rules.
